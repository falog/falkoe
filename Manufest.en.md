# Falkoe Manifesto

Falkoe is not a pitch-accent app.

The only thing we can say for sure is this:  
the first place where your voice reveals who it really is  
just happened to be pitch accent.

---

## What Falkoe Does Not Do

- Correct your pronunciation
- Make you memorize pitch-accent patterns
- Tell you “just imitate it and you’ll be fine”

---

## What Falkoe Does

- Expose the moments where your voice is lying
- Make them visible as deviations in F0 and timing
- Refuse to gently hand you the correct answer

---

## Why Pitch Accent?

Because it is where your voice cannot cheat.

Your ears may forgive you,  
but your voice will not.

What matters is not whether you said it “correctly,”  
but the moment you tried to escape.
