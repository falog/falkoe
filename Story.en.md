# Falkoe Story

Memorization was never my strength.

For a long time, I struggled with “remembering” things.
Then one day, I tried studying vocabulary using recordings of my own voice.

The result surprised me.
The words stayed.

I realized that my own voice had more weight than I expected.

In language learning, facing your own voice is unavoidable.
You can ignore mistakes on paper.
You can misunderstand what you hear.
But your own voice reveals what actually happened.

That realization led me to build a language learning tool centered on voice.

With this tool, you can:

- record your own speech
- revisit it later
- observe what actually occurred

Model voices.
Your voice.
And my voice.

A place where voices for language learning gather.

This is what I call “Fal-ification.”
